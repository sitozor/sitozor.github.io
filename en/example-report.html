Important

Your domain's email is not protected from spoofing (no DMARC)

What it means
Fraudsters can send email that appears to come from your domain, such as fake invoices to customers. Your real emails are also more likely to land in spam.
What to do
Add a DMARC DNS record: start in monitoring mode (p=none) with a reporting address, then move to p=quarantine or p=reject. This is done in your DNS provider's panel.
